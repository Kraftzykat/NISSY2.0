The National Insurance Scheme protects workers and families through 19 benefits — Age Pension, Survivors, Invalidity, Sickness, Maternity, Unemployment, Funeral Grant, and Employment Injury (Death, Disablement, Medical Expenses).
Employees pay 6.25% and employers 7.25%, for a total of 13.5% of insurable earnings. Self-employed people pay 13.5%, and voluntary contributors 6.75%. The rate rises gradually to 16% by 2031. Contributions are paid on up to $5,200/month ($1,200/week).
The pensionable age is currently 63 (2026), increasing to 65 by 2028. You need at least 575 contribution weeks (about 11.5 years). The Age Pension is 27% of your best 5 years' average earnings, up to 60%, with a minimum of $58/month.
Sickness Benefit pays 65% of your average insurable earnings for up to 26 weeks (up to 52 weeks for long-time contributors). You must be registered at least 3 months and have at least 2 months' contributions before the sick leave.
Unemployment Benefit pays 50% of your average weekly insurable earnings for up to 13 weeks. You must have been registered and contributing for at least 52 weeks, with contributions in the weeks before losing your job.
Survivors Benefit provides a monthly pension if the deceased had 150+ contributions, or a one-time grant if they had 50+. Minimums: widow(er)/parent $58/month, child/orphan $29/month. A Funeral Grant also helps cover funeral costs — claim within 6 months of the death.
Yes. Self-employed people pay 13.5% of gross earnings and can qualify for every benefit. You may pay between $907 and $8,424 a year, and you must contribute in the current year to qualify for benefits the following year.
Register and sign in to the NIS web portal at my.nisgrenada.org, call (473) 440-6647, or visit the office on Melville St, St George's (Mon–Fri, 7:30am–4:30pm).
Submit the relevant claim form to the NIS office. Watch the deadlines: sickness claims within 3 months of sick leave, funeral grants within 6 months of death. Nissy can help you find the right form and next steps.
